Cookie Policy

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files placed on your device by websites. Similar technologies can also store or read information from your browser or device.

How This Website Uses Cookies

This website does not intentionally set advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. It uses third-party services to load fonts and front-end assets, which may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the page requested.

Essential Technologies

Some technical processing is necessary to deliver the website, maintain security, and display pages correctly. Essential technologies do not require optional consent where they are strictly necessary for the service you request.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work, although this website is designed to remain accessible without optional cookies.

Future Changes

If we add analytics, advertising, embedded media, finance widgets, or other tools that set non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and provide a consent option where required.
