Privacy Policy

Who We Are

Prestige and Performance Cars operates from Unit 14 Astra Business Park, Guinness Road, Manchester, M17 1SD. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you contact us, visit our website, enquire about a vehicle, or buy from us.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, contact details, enquiry details, vehicle interests, purchase information, payment and identity checks where required, correspondence, and website usage information. If a third party introduces you to us, we may receive relevant enquiry or sales information from them.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, arrange viewings, prepare sales documents, manage payments, meet legal and accounting obligations, handle complaints, improve our website, and keep appropriate business records. We only send marketing where we have a lawful basis to do so, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Lawful Bases

Depending on the context, we process information because it is needed to take steps before entering a contract, perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, protect our legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent.

Sharing Information

We may share information with professional advisers, payment providers, IT and website suppliers, vehicle history or verification providers, warranty or delivery partners where applicable, regulators, law enforcement, and other parties where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, including legal, tax, accounting, complaint-handling, and record-keeping requirements. Enquiry records that do not lead to a sale are reviewed periodically and deleted when no longer needed.

Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of your personal information. You can also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To raise a privacy request, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to Prestige and Performance Cars, Unit 14 Astra Business Park, Guinness Road, Manchester, M17 1SD, or call +44 7447 053504.
